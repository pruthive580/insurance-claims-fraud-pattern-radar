The “provably right” half

Every score is an itemised receipt

Why it matters: no probability to argue with — every point cites a named rule, and they simply add up. That's what makes a decline or an SIU referral defensible.
The “adaptive” half

Three jobs for AI — none of them the verdict

Why it compounds

The rulebook gets smarter — safely

The moat: most AI fraud tools drift and can't explain themselves. Here the engine improves with your book, every change is a human-approved, auditable rule.
What single-claim review can't see

Organised fraud = connected claims

In plain terms: three different people, but the same phone, shop and lawyer. That shared box tying strangers together is the ring — and it's the highest-value fraud to catch.
